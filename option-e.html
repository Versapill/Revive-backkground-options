Who do you service?

Everyone! We welcome every client, whether it's your first lash set or your monthly facial.

What services do you offer?

Eyelash extensions, microblading, lip blushing, facials, face rejuvenation, acne treatment, chemical peels, microdermabrasion, waxing and tattoo removal, plus premium products for sale. Looking for something not listed? Contact our friendly team.

Why should I choose Revive Aesthetics OC?

We are a well reviewed, well respected, top rated spa with great reviews on all of the major review platforms. Our team is extremely well trained, certified and experienced, and our standards of care are high.

Can you fix lashes done at another studio?

Yes. If another artist placed too much glue or grouped too many lashes, we can fix it when possible so you're happy with your lashes for any occasion.

How long does microblading last?

Microblading typically lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on your skin type and skincare routine.

When should I arrive for my appointment?

Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment so you have time to fill out any paperwork.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Life happens. Text or call and you'll reach a real person, even on closed days. We won't penalize you for less than 24 hours' notice and almost never charge a cancellation fee. We'll work with you to reschedule.

Are walk-ins welcome?

No, please make an appointment. We have many wonderful clients, and to serve you properly you'll need an appointment.

What are your safety standards?

We use hospital-grade sterilization and sanitation methods for your safety and peace of mind.
